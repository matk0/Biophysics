# Biophysics

This vault contains concepts for studying biophysics and the meaningful connections between them.

## Current synthesis

The current knowledge base connects three developing threads. [[Concepts/fats|Fats]] and [[Concepts/fatty-acids|fatty acids]] introduce biological lipids, their structural and energetic roles, and their use as mitochondrial fuel. [[Concepts/serotonin|Serotonin]] and [[Concepts/melatonin|melatonin]] form a context-dependent pathway shaped by circadian timing, light, temperature, nutrients, and the microbiome. [[Concepts/mitochondrial-magnetism|Mitochondrial magnetism]] connects electron flow, oxygen delivery, cold, uncoupling, melatonin, and fatty acids; fatty acids and melatonin therefore bridge the lipid, neurochemical, and mitochondrial branches.
