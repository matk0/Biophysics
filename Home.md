# Biophysics

This vault contains concepts for studying biophysics and the meaningful connections between them.

## Current synthesis

The current knowledge base connects six developing threads. [[Concepts/hydrogen|Hydrogen]] introduces atomic states and probability densities. [[Concepts/fats|Fats]] and [[Concepts/fatty-acids|fatty acids]] introduce biological lipids, their structural and energetic roles, and their use as mitochondrial fuel. [[Concepts/serotonin|Serotonin]] and [[Concepts/melatonin|melatonin]] form a context-dependent pathway shaped by circadian timing, light, temperature, nutrients, and the microbiome. [[Concepts/mitochondrial-magnetism|Mitochondrial magnetism]] connects electron flow, oxygen delivery, cold, uncoupling, melatonin, and fatty acids. [[Concepts/amino-acids|Amino acids]] and [[Concepts/proteins|proteins]] connect nutrition to [[Concepts/anabolism|anabolism]], [[Concepts/catabolism|catabolism]], and [[Concepts/photosynthesis|photosynthesis]], while [[Concepts/dna|DNA]], [[Concepts/genes|genes]], and [[Concepts/chromosomes|chromosomes]] connect biological information to [[Concepts/protein-synthesis|protein synthesis]].
