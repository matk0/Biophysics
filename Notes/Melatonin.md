# Melatonin

Melatonin is produced from [[Serotonin|serotonin]] and released in darkness according to circadian timing. Its production and use are coordinated by the light-dark cycle, temperature, the microbiome, DHA, EPA, and sulfate rather than by precursor availability alone.

Melatonin lowers body temperature, supports UCP-mediated uncoupling, and protects and repairs mitochondria. During sleep, [[Fatty acids|fatty acids]] released from subcutaneous fat sustain electron flow, heat, and metabolic water, connecting melatonin to [[Mitochondrial magnetism|mitochondrial magnetism]].
