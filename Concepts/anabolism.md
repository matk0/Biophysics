# Anabolism

## English

Anabolism is the assembly of larger molecules from smaller ones. In the chapter it describes building processes such as joining [[Concepts/amino-acids|amino acids]] into muscle proteins, and is associated with a positive nitrogen balance. Anabolism and [[Concepts/catabolism|catabolism]] occur at the same time in different parts of the body.

## Slovenčina

Anabolizmus je skladanie väčších molekúl z menších. V kapitole opisuje budovacie procesy, napríklad spájanie [[Concepts/amino-acids|aminokyselín]] do svalových bielkovín, a spája sa s pozitívnou dusíkovou bilanciou. Anabolizmus a [[Concepts/catabolism|katabolizmus]] prebiehajú v rôznych častiach tela súčasne.
