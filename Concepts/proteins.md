# Proteins

## English

Proteins are structural and functional molecules built from chains of [[Concepts/amino-acids|amino acids]]. The body uses them when it builds and repairs muscles, skin, organs, enzymes, neurons, memories, and hormones. Among the three [[Concepts/macronutrients|macronutrients]], proteins are distinguished in the chapter by containing nitrogen as well as hydrogen, oxygen, and carbon.

## Slovenčina

Bielkoviny sú stavebné a funkčné molekuly vytvorené z reťazcov [[Concepts/amino-acids|aminokyselín]]. Telo ich používa pri budovaní a oprave svalov, pokožky, orgánov, enzýmov, neurónov, pamäte a hormónov. Spomedzi troch [[Concepts/macronutrients|makronutrientov]] sa bielkoviny v kapitole odlišujú tým, že okrem vodíka, kyslíka a uhlíka obsahujú aj dusík.
