# Macronutrients

## English

Macronutrients are the major nutrient groups that make up food: carbohydrates, [[Concepts/proteins|proteins]], and fats. Their proportions are often used to describe a diet, while the amount of [[Concepts/micronutrients|micronutrients]] in the same food remains important as well.

## Slovenčina

Makronutrienty sú hlavné skupiny živín, z ktorých sa skladá potrava: sacharidy, [[Concepts/proteins|bielkoviny]] a tuky. Ich pomer sa často používa na opis jedálnička, no rovnako dôležité zostáva množstvo [[Concepts/micronutrients|mikronutrientov]] v tej istej potrave.
