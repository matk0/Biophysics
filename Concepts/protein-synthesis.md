# Protein synthesis

## English

Protein synthesis is the process by which a cell uses genetic information to assemble a protein. A required segment of [[Concepts/dna|DNA]] is made accessible and its information is transcribed into RNA; a ribosome then joins [[Concepts/amino-acids|amino acids]] in the encoded order while the DNA remains preserved as the template.

## Slovenčina

Syntéza bielkovín je proces, pri ktorom bunka používa genetickú informáciu na zostavenie bielkoviny. Potrebný úsek [[Concepts/dna|DNA]] sa sprístupní a jeho informácia sa prepíše do RNA; ribozóm potom spája [[Concepts/amino-acids|aminokyseliny]] v zakódovanom poradí, zatiaľ čo DNA zostáva zachovaná ako predloha.
