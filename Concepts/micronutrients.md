# Micronutrients

## English

Micronutrients are smaller components of food such as vitamins, minerals, and trace elements. Foods can contain both [[Concepts/macronutrients|macronutrients]] and many micronutrients; the chapter contrasts nutrient-dense whole foods with highly processed foods that provide substantial energy but few micronutrients.

## Slovenčina

Mikronutrienty sú menšie zložky potravy, napríklad vitamíny, minerály a stopové prvky. Potraviny môžu obsahovať [[Concepts/macronutrients|makronutrienty]] aj množstvo mikronutrientov; kapitola porovnáva výživovo bohaté celé potraviny s vysoko spracovanými potravinami, ktoré dodávajú veľa energie, ale málo mikronutrientov.
